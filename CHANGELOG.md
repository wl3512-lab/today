# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (first public version)

- Today: menu bar planner with Today / Study / Classes / History pages, homework
  briefs, Plan it (sessions placed around classes and your calendar), the Study
  Kit (links, apps, Focus, playlist, 25/5 timer) and grade and attendance tracking.
- Quiz Prep: a study kit per quiz from your slides, photo-to-practice, score log.
  Uses your own Claude Code when there is no class AI proxy.
- Connectors: Brightspace (any school) and Canvas (personal access token), plus
  Campuswire.
- Setup: Claude interviews you about your classes (`CLAUDE-SETUP.md`), writes
  your settings and installs everything with `tools/install.sh`.
